- ROT field 7 is split: `hipotese_tratamento` = 7A (art. 7º, dado pessoal, always required) and `hipotese_sensivel` = 7B (art. 11, required only when a dado pessoal sensível is listed, cleared otherwise); legacy rows with art. 11 in 7A are migrated on edit. Rules live in `src/lib/priva/rot.ts` and the DB trigger. Why: LGPD separates arts. 7º and 11.
- National holidays for ANPD deadline counting live in `src/lib/priva/feriados.ts` and are mirrored in the `limite_anpd_uteis` DB function; extend both together per year. Why: client counter and server lateness flag must agree.
- ROT field 1 is the "Área › Setor" hierarchy (`areasSetores.ts`): 7 fixed areas with suggested sectors plus "outro"; the area's short key feeds the `setor` column that generates PRIVA-<SETOR>-NNN, and the full hierarchy is stored in `area_setor`. Why: preserve existing IDs and rows while showing the method's hierarchy.
- ROT field 2 renders in two stages (`ETAPA1`/`ETAPA2` in `cicloVida.ts`): perimeter (Coletar/Reter/Processar) and destination (Compartilhar/Eliminar); `destinoDeclarado` gates PI-02 sign-off and `alertaRetencao` warns on retention without destination. Why: organizational grouping only — the 20 verbs of art. 5º, X stay intact.
- Deterministic suggestions live in the versioned `SUGESTOES_FINALIDADE_V1` table in `rot.ts` (never AI, never pre-selected, always "Sugerido — confirme" with origin); `alertasCoerencia` holds the non-blocking data×hypótese alerts. Why: auditability and no false evidence.
- `membros_empresa` stores member CPF/CNPJ outside the SELECT grant; reads go through the audited `revelar_documento_membro` RPC, deletion is logical only, and the first insert per company auto-creates the governance ROT row via trigger. Why: LGPD arts. 6º (VII/VIII), 37 and 46 — document segregation with forensic trail.
